
# Fatema Tuz Zuhra
### Urban Planner | Environmental Researcher | Geospatial Data Analyst

I am an Urban and Regional Planner and Researcher currently pursuing a Master of Urban and Regional Planning at Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET). My research explores the intersection of urban development, environmental sustainability, climate change, and geospatial science.

My work focuses on applying Remote Sensing, GIS, spatial statistics, and machine learning to investigate urban and environmental challenges. I am particularly interested in understanding spatial patterns, environmental vulnerability, ecosystem dynamics, and the implications of urban transformation.

I enjoy combining geospatial technologies with quantitative research methods to generate evidence that supports sustainable planning and policy decisions.

**Research interests:** Urban Sustainability · Environmental Modelling · Climate Science · Ecosystem Stability · Spatial Data Science · Remote Sensing

## Research Portfolio

My research spans urban and environmental planning, climate science, geospatial analysis, and spatial modelling. I work with multi-source geospatial datasets, remote sensing, statistical modelling, and machine learning to investigate complex urban and environmental challenges.

### Ongoing Research

**1. Influence of Built Environment Characteristics on Urban Comfort in Dhaka (2026)**

- Integrating perception surveys with GIS and remote sensing-derived built environment metrics.
- Psychometric validation and spatial analysis.
- Applying machine learning to evaluate the relative contributions of urban form characteristics to perceived urban comfort.

**2. Assessment of Ecosystem Stability in Coastal Tidal Flats: A Systematic Review of Frameworks and an Empirical Application to the Coast of Bangladesh (MURP Thesis, BUET)**

- Developing a multidimensional socio-ecological framework for assessing coastal ecosystem stability.
- Integrating environmental, socioeconomic, and governance indicators.
- Combining systematic literature review, conceptual framework development, geospatial analysis, and statistical assessment.

**3. Urban Growth and Public Infrastructure Allocation: Are Growing Urban Areas Receiving Proportional Fiscal Support? A Measurement and Prediction for 12 City Corporations in Bangladesh**

*Funded by the Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office (FCDO).*

- Processing 500+ Landsat images in Google Earth Engine.
- Developing 72 multi-temporal LULC maps (2020–2025) across 12 city corporations.
- Forecasting urban expansion to 2030 using Land Change Modeler (TerrSet).
- Assessing infill, edge and leapfrog growth, urban growth efficiency, compactness, and environmental pressure.
- Developing an integrated urban sustainability priority index.

### Submitted Research (2026)

**4. Hybrid GIS–Machine Learning Framework for Waste-to-Energy Site Selection in Dhaka**

- Spatial data preprocessing and feature engineering using QGIS and Google Earth Engine.
- Weighted overlay of 12 criteria using AHP, raster suitability modelling, and constraint masking.
- Implementing five supervised machine learning algorithms.
- Model optimisation using GridSearchCV and five-fold cross-validation, followed by interpretable model evaluation.

**5. Unraveling the Anthropogenic Drivers of PM₂.₅ Concentration in Dhaka**

- Integrating population, night-time lights, LULC, building height, road proximity, slum proximity, and brick kiln influence.
- Random Forest optimisation using Bayesian optimisation.
- Feature importance analysis, Structural Equation Modelling (SEM), and Geographically Weighted Regression (GWR).

**6. Assessment Frameworks for Ecosystem Stability: A Systematic Literature Review**

- PRISMA 2020-guided systematic literature review using Scopus.
- Screening of 6,209 records, supplemented by backward snowballing.
- Qualitative coding and thematic synthesis of ecosystem stability frameworks.
- Comparative assessment of stability dimensions, indicators, disturbance types, and methodological approaches.

**7. Impact of Agricultural Drought and Climate Change on Rice Yields in Northwestern Bangladesh (2025)**

- Drought monitoring using SPEI and PDSI (2000–2022).
- Integration of BRRI rice yield data and climate variables derived from Google Earth Engine.
- Generalized Additive Modelling (GAM) to investigate nonlinear climate–yield relationships.
- Multiple Linear Regression for prediction and validation.

### Previous Research & Thesis Projects

**8. Mega Project Impact on Urban Growth and Ecosystem Services (BURP Thesis, KUET | 2021–2022)**

- Assessing the impact of the Padma Bridge on urban growth and LULC dynamics.
- Evaluating changes in ecosystem service values in the surrounding region.
- Applying Multi-Layer Perceptron–Markov modelling (MLP-MCA).
- Identifying ecological trade-offs associated with infrastructure-led urban development.
